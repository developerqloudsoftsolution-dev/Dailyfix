Engineered Specifically for Men's Coarse Facial Hair and Sensitive Skin

Facial hair follicles are naturally thicker, coarser, and more resistant to standard colouring treatments than regular scalp hair, which often results in patchy or overly dark, unnatural finishes when using traditional box dyes.

Dailyfix overcomes these challenges through an advanced micro-pigment delivery system enriched with organic olive extract, taurine, and vital botanical conditioning agents that actively fortify each hair strand from root to tip.

The soothing, dermatologically tested formulation completely eliminates harsh chemical odours and scalp itching, allowing gentlemen to achieve professional, salon-calibre grooming results comfortably in their own homes.
